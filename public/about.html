About Smokeyz BBQ

Smokeyz BBQ builds custom smokers out of Freeport, Texas. Every unit, whether it's an offset, barrel, pellet, hybrid, or kettle design, is fabricated to order rather than pulled off a factory line.

We work directly with each customer on sizing, wood selection, finish, and hardware, so the smoker that arrives is built around how you actually cook, not a generic template.

Have questions about a custom build? Reach out and we'll walk you through the options.
